HOW TO ENROLL

Three steps to get started.

A simple path from choosing your program to submitting your application.

Browse programs ↗
02

Prepare your documents

03

Submit your application

Visit the admissions office with your documents and ask the team about the next enrollment steps.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

What to prepare.

Requirements may vary by program. Confirm the latest checklist with the admissions office before submitting your application.

Helpful documents to bring

  • Completed student application form
  • Latest report card or transcript of records
  • Birth certificate or valid identification
  • Recent ID photos
  • Additional documents requested for your program

Have a question? Contact or visit the school admissions office for current dates, fees, and complete requirements.
